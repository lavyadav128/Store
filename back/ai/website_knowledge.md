# NoteNova — Platform Knowledge Base

This document describes the NoteNova platform itself, so the AI Study Copilot
can answer questions about the website, its features, and how to use it —
not just questions about study content.

## What is NoteNova?

NoteNova is an EdTech platform offering structured courses ("batches"),
notes, mentorship, and doubt-solving support across multiple categories:

- IIT JEE preparation (Class 10, 11, 12)
- Jee Mains preparation (Class 10, 11, 12, including NEET-focused tracks)
- College engineering subjects (Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Engineering)
- CDS / SSB Interview preparation
- Tech skills: DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms), Web Development, Data Analysis, Aptitude Preparation

## How Batches Work

A "batch" is a structured course a student can purchase or register for.
Each batch includes subjects, and each subject contains chapters. Chapters
may include: mind maps, short notes, complete notes, and instructional videos.

Some batches are free (price: 0) and some are paid (a one-time payment via
Razorpay). Once purchased or registered, access lasts for approximately
10 months from the date of purchase/registration. After this period, the
student needs to re-purchase the batch to regain access — access does not
auto-renew.

## Notes Section

Separately from Batches, NoteNova has a dedicated Notes section, organized
by batch → subject → chapter, with downloadable short notes, complete
notes, and mind maps for each chapter.

## How to Purchase a Batch

1. Browse batches by category from the dashboard.
2. Click on a batch to see its details and price.
3. Click "Buy Now" — this opens Razorpay's secure payment popup.
4. After successful payment, the batch is instantly added to "My Purchases"
   and the student gets full access to its content.
5. A WhatsApp confirmation message is sent automatically after a successful paid purchase.

## Doubt-Solving Support

Students can submit a doubt (a question) with a subject and message from
their dashboard. Admins/mentors review and reply to doubts. When a mentor
replies, the student receives an instant real-time notification (no need
to refresh the page) and can also see it later on their Notifications page.

## AI Study Copilot (this chatbot)

Students can ask this AI assistant questions about their study material —
it searches the relevant notes/chapters and answers using that content.
Students can also upload their own PDF for the AI to summarize on the spot.

## Test Series & PYQ (Previous Year Questions)

Premium batches include access to a Test Series (practice tests with
tracked attempts and scoring) and a PYQ Series (previous year exam questions).

## Account & Login

Students sign up with their name, email, and a password. Google Sign-In
is also fully supported as an alternative login method.

## Support & Contact

For any issue the AI Study Copilot cannot resolve, students should use the
in-app Doubt system to reach a mentor/admin directly. This is currently the
only support channel — there is no separate email or phone support line.

## Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refunds for paid batches are available within 3 days of purchase. After
this 3-day window, the purchase is final and non-refundable.

## Pricing

Pricing varies per batch and is set individually by the admin — some
batches are free, others are paid one-time purchases (commonly in the
₹9–₹99 range based on current offerings, but always check the specific
batch page for the exact current price, since prices can change).
