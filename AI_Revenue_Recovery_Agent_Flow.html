1. Ingestion & Dual Fast-Path Reporting

When an OTP fails or the student dismisses checkout, recoveryClient.js posts to /api/recovery/payment-failed, while Razorpay sends an HMAC-signed webhook. This dual capture ensures 0% signal loss.

Files: recoveryClient.js, webhook.js Security: HMAC-SHA256 Status: open

2. AI Diagnostic Cognition (Groq Llama-3.3-70b)

Groq's Llama-3.3-70b processes the failure in ~250ms, diagnoses the cause (bank_otp_timeout), calculates confidence (0.95), and writes a personalized Hinglish voice script offering a 10% discount.

File: reasoningService.js Fallback: OpenRouter Nemotron / Deterministic Matrix

3. Policy Gate (Auto-Approve vs HITL Queue)

Signals $\le ₹5,000$ are auto-approved. High-value transactions ($> ₹5,000$) are routed to the Human-in-the-Loop Admin Queue (/approval-queue) for mandatory 1-click admin sign-off.

File: gateService.js Rules: 10% max discount, 3 retries limit, 15m cooldown

4. Verified Recovery & Real-Time Financial ROI

When the student pays via the 24-hour voucher, Razorpay confirms the transaction. The agent attributes the revenue, updates the Net Profit margin (Gross - Discount), and unlocks course access instantly.

File: webhook.js, routes.js (/metrics) Result: +₹4,999 Gross | ₹500 Discount | +₹4,499 Net Profit
